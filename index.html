Fitness Logger

A personal tool that logs daily step counts to a team fitness channel.

What it does

Fitness Logger reads my own daily step count from my Google Health account and submits it to my team’s Slack fitness workflow, so I don’t have to type it in by hand.

Who it’s for

It is a personal project, built and used by one person (Kartik). It is not a public service and has no sign-up.

Data it uses

Read-only access to step counts (Google Health activity and fitness data). Nothing is written back to Google.
